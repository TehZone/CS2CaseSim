The first version with updates built in: from now on the game tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you.

- Online play is now on one server: there's no address to enter, just log in.
- You need the latest version to play online with other players. Offline play always works.
- Name Tags and StatTrak Swap Tools, with CS2's own rules and the name plate on your gun in 3D.
- Storage Units: keep up to 1,000 items out of your inventory, offline and online.
- Index: a new tab with everything you've unboxed, and rewards for finishing cases and collections.
- Charms behave as they do in CS2, with a proper turn in the inspect. The 8 Ball answers when shaken, the lighter's liquid sloshes, and every charm shows its own pattern's colours on its card.
- The Market shows every charm's pattern number, so rare patterns are easy to find.
- Open or buy 10 cases at once, and the reel stays full on both sides however many you open.
- Play: you play as the agent you have equipped. Console cheats work in private matches only, and a match with cheats in it pays nothing.
- Changelog: a button at the top right with every update to the game.
