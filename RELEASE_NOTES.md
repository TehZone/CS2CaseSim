Play online against other players.

- Play: queue online for Competitive or Wingman against real players (Play → Online). A full lobby starts at once; after 2 minutes, everyone searching is split into two teams and bots fill the empty slots. The player with the best connection hosts the match.
- Play: online matches pay like bot matches: wins, kills, assists and MVPs.
- Play: if you drop out of an online match, a bot plays for you until you rejoin.
- Play: spectating after you die is first person only.
