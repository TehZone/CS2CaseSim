The first version with updates built in: from now on the game tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you.

- Online play is now on one server: there's no address to enter, just log in.
- You need the latest version to play online with other players. Offline play always works.
- Storage Units: keep up to 1,000 items out of your inventory, offline and online.
- Charms behave as they do in CS2, with a proper turn in the inspect.
