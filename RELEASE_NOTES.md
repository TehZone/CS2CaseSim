- Cases: its rare special items are four new knife finishes, Magma, Toxic Waste, Reactor and Radioactive Doppler (Phase 1 to 4, Ruby and Sapphire), on the Bayonet, Butterfly Knife, Flip Knife, Gut Knife, Huntsman Knife, Karambit and M9 Bayonet.
- Trade-ups: the guns of The Nuclear Collection trade up into each other, and five of its Coverts into one of its knives.
