Badges you can turn in your hands, and a Service Medal for every prestige.

- Badges: inspecting a badge shows its real CS2 model (pins, coins, Service Medals, Premier medals and trophies) with CS2's metal, colours and glass. It needs CS2 installed; passes and tokens show their picture.
- Prestige: every prestige gives you its Service Medal badge, including prestiges you've already earned, online and offline. Medals can't be traded or sold, don't count toward the inventory limit, and can be featured on your profile picture.
