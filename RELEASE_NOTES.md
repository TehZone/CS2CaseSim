Rounder smokes, coloured names and a smaller CS2 cache.

- Play: a smoke is one round dome instead of a pyramid, and one popped on a ledge, box, stairs or roof edge pours down to the ground instead of floating in the air.
- Inventory: colour your Storage Unit labels (free, change it any time) and your Name Tag names (picked when you apply the tag). Other players see the colours too.
- Settings: "Maps only" next to Prepare all exports just the maps you can play and their sounds, so a match on any of them starts right away.
- Settings: "Compress the CS2 cache" (on by default) has Windows keep the cache's maps, models and sounds compressed on your drive, about a quarter smaller, and unpack them in memory as the game reads them. It never runs during a match.
- Settings: Prepare all no longer exports the hostage maps Play doesn't use (about 2.8 GB), and old exports of them are deleted. Map skies and textures the game shrinks take less space.
- Play: private match tiles say "Ready to play" for maps already prepared.
